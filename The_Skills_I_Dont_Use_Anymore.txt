The Skills I Don’t Use Anymore

Chapter 0
How I Came to That Stage

I did not wake up one day and decide to become that guy.
There was no plan. No intention.

Sach ye hai ki main bas akela ho gaya tha.
Aur uske saath ek kind of emptiness bhi aa gayi thi.
Life me ek naya emotion discover kiya tha maine,
aur phir dheere-dheere main us emotion ko miss karne laga 
jaise kisi ne bina warning wo cheez mujhse chheen li ho.

After teenage, jab tum attention ko samajhne lagte ho,
tumhe uski value feel hone lagti hai.
When someone notices you, listens to you, chooses you 
you start falling in love with that feeling.

Aur phir tum bhi wahi attention wapas dene lagte ho.
Kabhi thoda zyada, kabhi thoda kam.
Sometimes consciously, sometimes without even realising it.

Par kabhi-kabhi ye flow achanak break ho jata hai.
Kuch reasons ki wajah se.
Kuch lies ki wajah se.
Aur kuch cases me, cheating ki wajah se.

Ye meri life ka wahi starting phase tha
jab mujhe seekhna pada:

how to talk to someone

how to express yourself

and how to present who you really are

Kabhi-kabhi main khud ko doosron ki nazar se dekhne laga.
Isko na bilkul galat bol sakte hain, na bilkul sahi.
It’s just a phase almost everyone goes through.

Koi bhi insaan factory se perfect hoke nahi aata.
Aur na hi life ke end tak jaakar koi perfect ban jata hai.
We all are learning, one phase at a time.

Us phase ko cross karte hue,
maine dating ke baare me seekha,
logon ko samajhna seekha,
aur emotions ko observe karna seekha.

Mujhe laga shayad agar main apni learning likh doon,
to koi aur apne phase me khud ko thoda kam lost feel kare.
Maybe it helps someone understand themselves better.

Is book me jo tips aur tricks likhi hain,
unke saath unka real outcome bhi likha gaya hai 
without filters, without pretending.

Main nahi chahta ki tum in cheezon ka use sabke saath karo.
I just want you to understand them, not blindly follow them.

So…
let us start this journey.

Chapter 1
How You Should Pursue Any Person

Now, if you are finally ready to pursue someone for yourself, then first understand one thing:

## There is a very big pond in this world, believe me.

And when I say this line literally, I mean it!
It is a huge pond full of so many fish, and anyone can find the one they want.

The only thing that matters is:

## How you want to approach them and how much effort you are willing to make for them.

But before approaching anyone, ask yourself one question:

## “What exactly do I want from this person?”

Because many times, people enter relationships without even understanding what they actually need.

Are you looking for:

Dating?

Support?

A talking stage?

Someone who truly understands you?

Or are you just trying to fill an empty space in your life?

Once you are clear about what you need from another person, you automatically start understanding yourself and your emotions better.

Kyu ki kai baar insaan samajh hi nahi pata ki usko pyaar chahiye, dost chahiye, sahara chahiye, ya bas koi saath chahiye.

Aur isi confusion mein woh bas ye sochta rehta hai:

“Yaar bas koi mil jaye.”

Aur jab koi mil jata hai, woh apne emotions ke confusion mein aa kar us bane hue rishte ko barbaad kar deta hai.

So before pursuing someone, first learn how to control yourself and understand yourself.

Let your mind first find peace within itself, then go for someone.

Because in dating, if you cannot handle yourself, you will become like a bomb sometimes hurting yourself, and sometimes hurting the other person too.

Khud ko samjho. Khud ko heal karo. Khud ko ready karo.

Work on yourself.

Aur yaha pe work on yourself ka matlab perfect banna nahi hai.

Yaha pe khud par kaam karne ka matlab hai understanding yourself, self-talk karna, aur apne emotions ko samajhna.

Khud ke loopholes ko heal karo.

Un cheezon par kaam karo jin par tum jaldi hype up ho jate ho, ya jin cheezon ko koi aur tumhare against use kar sakta hai.

Aur ye baat tumhe aage samajh aayegi ki tumhare loopholes kaise dusron ke liye useful ho sakte hain.

## Now Start Pursuing Someone for Yourself

Different people are different in their hobbies, interests, and the way they spend their time.

Believe me, the question we used to ask in childhood:

## “What are your hobbies?”

is actually one of the best questions to understand someone’s personality.

How they like to spend their time, what they enjoy doing when they are free, and how they choose to live in their quiet moments — these things tell you a lot about a person.

Because somewhere, these habits reflect:

how they think

how they react

and sometimes even how they treat people

And trust me, hobbies tell much more about a person than people usually realise.

## First, Talk About the People Who Love Books or Films

Your next step after asking about hobbies should be understanding the stories they consume.

Ask them:

What do they read?

What films do they enjoy?

What kind of stories do they love?

And when you get names of books or movies, do not ignore them.

Grab their summaries and try to understand the things that are common between those stories.

Kyu ki wahi common point tumhe us person ke important emotions ke baare mein batayega — ki woh kis tarah ki stories dekh ya padh kar khud ko shape karta hai.

Believe me, many people who read books or watch films deeply connect themselves with certain characters.

Sometimes, if you just get to know their favourite character and understand that character properly, you automatically get many things to talk about with them.

At the same time, you also begin to understand:

how they react to situations

what emotionally affects them

and what kind of mindset they may have

Kyu ki bahut log stories ko apni real life mein follow karte hain.

Woh khud ko un characters ki jagah rakh kar sochte hain.

Isliye, understanding this side of human thinking becomes very important.

Jo log stories, films, ya books consume karte hain, woh aksar khud ko better express kar pate hain.

Because somewhere, they learn expression through:

words

dialogues

emotions

and gestures

Unhe pata hota hai naraz kaise hona hai, emotions kaise show karne hain, aur kab kya feel karna hai — stories unhe ye sab sikhati hain.

Bas yaha tumhe unki pasand ki stories ko samajhna hoga, taaki tum unke expression aur unke tareeke ko better samajh sako.

## Second, Talk About the People Who Love to Paint or Draw

Believe me, they are usually not extroverts until and unless they feel comfortable with you.

Because art is not always a direct medium of expressing feelings through words.

Woh har baat ko bol kar express nahi kar pate.

Sometimes, their hobby becomes the place where they express what they actually feel.

And believe me, people with an art hobby often hold really interesting personalities.

I do not know from what perspective you will see it — it can be anything you cannot even imagine.

But for sure, if you find the right one, they will show you how things can look crazy and still make sense at the same time.

If you observe artistic people, many of them are dreamers.

But not the kind of dreamers who always show the world what they want to become.

They are more like people who want to complete something deeply personal — something that may only make sense to them.

Stories and films are often made for others to understand, which is why they may feel more open and expressive.

But art is different.

## Art feels personal.

Sometimes, artists do not even care if everyone understands what they are trying to say.

Their work is not always made to explain things.

Sometimes, it is simply made to feel things.

But if you want to experience something deep, unexpected, and sometimes beautifully crazy 
believe me, these kinds of people can show you shades of life that you never expected to see.

## Third, Talk About the People Who Love Sports
