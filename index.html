The Manifesto

ENTRY № 01

Nightwalker is my way of collecting the pieces of a life that feels too much, too quiet, and too alive all at once.

A personal creative archive where literature meets mountains, where photographs hold conversations, and where stories find a home. It is not a conventional portfolio, generic blog, or template-based website. It is a memory room, a quiet rebellion, and a reminder that there is still beauty in the ordinary.

"Why do you walk these trails? Because down there, people tell you who you are supposed to be. How you should live, what you should earn. But up here... the mountains don't tell you anything. They just let you be."

— Anshul, on the trail to म्यूर पहाड़

Somewhere between high mountain passes and midnight silence, between questions that have no easy answers, I keep creating: to remember, to feel, and to return.
